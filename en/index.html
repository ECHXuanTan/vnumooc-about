Understanding the platform

What is the VNU-MOOC System?

The VNU-MOOC System is the digital learning platform of VNU-HCM, broadening learning opportunities in the digital space and accompanying students throughout their university journey.

A shared learning space connecting the knowledge of the VNU-HCM academic community with each student's needs, goals and learning pace.

Learning modes

Three modes of learning

01

Supplementary learning

Students proactively study more to reinforce their knowledge, fill gaps in what they have not yet mastered, or explore a new field.

02

Blended learning

Students study part of the content on the VNU-MOOC System, then continue with discussion, practice and interaction with lecturers in class.

03

Learning for credit

Students complete the module, take independent tests and assessments, and are considered for credit recognition and accumulation according to regulations.

Three modes of learning

  1. Supplementary materials: reinforce knowledge or explore new fields.
  2. Blended learning: online study combined with in-class discussion and practice.
  3. Study for credit: complete the module, take independent assessment, and be considered for recognition per regulations.

Credit recognition and transfer depend on the content, assessment results and each institution's regulations.
System access

Sign in to the VNU-MOOC System

To reach the right system, use the link and follow the sign-in instructions provided by your institution.

If you don't have access details yet, contact the support point at your institution.
Getting started

Start using the VNU-MOOC System

  1. Access the system following your institution's instructions.
  2. Explore the course: read the overview, syllabus and learning requirements.
  3. Study and practise: watch lessons, use materials and do interactive activities.
  4. Reach out when needed: ask questions in discussions or send a technical support request.

For modules intended for credit, follow your institution's separate guidance on registration, assessment and recognition.
Learner support

When you need technical support

While using the VNU-MOOC System you can send a request through the chat box on the system. Describe the issue and the content you're on so the team can help.

If you can't sign in, contact the support point at your institution.
